How to Apply for EQIP
  1. Contact your local USDA Service Center — farmers.gov
  2. Submit Conservation Program Application (NRCS-CPA-1200) — accepted year-round
  3. NRCS ranks applications by local resource concern priority; contracts awarded competitively
  4. Approved producers sign contract and implement practices per schedule
  5. Payment released upon NRCS verification of practice completion
  6. Beginning/underserved farmers: +25% payment rate and advance payment option (up to 50% before implementation)
  7. CSP (Conservation Stewardship Program): 5-year contracts covering a suite of practices including cover crops
